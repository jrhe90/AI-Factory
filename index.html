AI Factory

Real-time 3D digital twins of manufacturing plants. Every model is generated in the browser with three.js, with no assets and no build step. Lines, machines, people and vehicles run on a live flow simulation, with an AI layer on top.

New · AI digital twin

Electric Motor Plant

Modelled on ABB's Baldor-Reliance motor manufacturing. The plant layout follows a real floor plan: secondary processes, assembly area, east machining lines, warehouses, rewinding shop and welfare block.

  • 9 lines and 48 stations: stamping, die-casting, CNC, winding, VPI, run test, paint and pack
  • Forklifts, a tugger train, AGVs, trucks and dozens of animated people
  • AI predictive maintenance, vision QC camera feed, energy and risk heatmaps
  • Plant copilot that answers from live data, with optional Claude API
